About Us

Built on Engineering Excellence

Er. Ranjith, B.E. is a qualified Civil Engineer holding a Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) degree. As the lead engineer at Lakshmi Builder's & Planner's, he specializes in building construction, structural design, and project planning — delivering comprehensive services from conceptual design and structural analysis to on-site construction supervision.

Every project combines technical precision with practical field experience to create spaces that are safe, sustainable, and beautifully executed — guided by rigorous standards, transparent communication, and a commitment to timelines and budgets clients can rely on.
